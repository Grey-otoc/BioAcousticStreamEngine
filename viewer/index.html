BioAcoustic Stream Engine (BASE) · Blenheim Palace Innovation Team
How BASE Works
●
Live listeningMicrophones stream audio continuously from the estate, day and night.
●
Seeing soundEach sound becomes a spectrogram — a visual fingerprint of frequencies over time.
●
AI identificationMachine learning compares fingerprints to thousands of recordings and gives a species match with a confidence score.
●
Soil healthUnderground sensors record soil sound. Human noise is filtered out first, then the remaining acoustic activity is measured as a Soil Acoustic Index (SAI) — a direct indicator of microbial life and soil biodiversity.
●
Why it mattersBiodiversity underpins every living ecosystem. Knowing which species are present, and tracking how populations change over time, gives conservation teams the evidence they need to protect habitats.
